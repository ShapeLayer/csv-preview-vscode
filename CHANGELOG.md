# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Open a CSV preview in a tab or beside the editor with Ctrl+K, V (Cmd+K, V on macOS).
- Follow source scrolling and cursor movement, with optional active cell highlighting.
- Click a preview cell to navigate to its source position.
- Size columns automatically to fit their contents, or resize them by dragging column borders.
- Highlight the full visible column border while hovering or dragging.
- Reset all column widths from the preview's editor title menu.
- Follow VS Code theme colors and virtualize rows to limit rendered content.
- Support quoted commas, escaped quotes, multiline fields, BOM, and CRLF/LF/CR line endings.
